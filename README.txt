Pint's Threat Plates v1.1 - enemy nameplates coloured by threat
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsThreatPlates
  Restart the game.

WHAT YOU SEE
  Each enemy plate: the name above, a health bar with "1234/1234 | 56%" inside, the level on the
  right, your threat % on the left and a white edge on your target. Friendly units keep the
  game's own plates.

  The bar's colour is your threat on that mob:
    Not tanking - green (far from pulling it) -> yellow (about to pull) -> red (it's on you)
    Tanking     - dark red (safe lead) -> yellow (someone is catching up) -> orange (lost it - taunt)
  Tank colours switch on by themselves in Defensive Stance, Bear Form and Righteous Fury
  (or set Always / Never). "Flip tank colours" turns them round: green while you hold it.
  Colours change in smooth steps or in shades - your choice, and every colour can be changed.

  Threat arrows: a red up arrow on your highest-threat mob, a green down arrow on your lowest.
  Raid markers show on the plate.
  Stacking: plates move apart so none overlap, and stay on screen for large or close mobs.
  Already using TidyPlates Threat Plates? The options have a button to switch it off.

OPTIONS  (/pn, or click the minimap button)
  Show: name, level, threat %, threat arrows, raid markers, target edge
  Health text: numbers and %, numbers, % or nothing
  Bar width and height, name and bar text sizes, gap between plates, nameplate distance (yards)
  Tank colours: Automatic / Always / Never, flip, smooth or shaded, and all colours

COMMANDS
  /pn                open the options (also /threatplates)
  /pn tank           tank colours: automatic -> always -> never
  /pn range <yards>  how far away plates show (10-100)
  /pn width <px>     bar width (60-220)
  /pn minimap        show or hide the minimap button
