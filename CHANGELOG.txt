Pint's Threat Plates - Changelog

v1.3.5
- WoW Forever: mob cast bars fill smoothly (the game now runs them from the cast's own timer).

v1.3.4
- A grey (tagged) mob you have threat on gets a bold red edge, so you can see it's after you.
- Fixed "attempt to index field 'quest'" errors (and missing debuffs) when an old copy of the add-on was also
  installed: the two no longer share their plates.

v1.3.3
- A mob tagged by someone else now turns grey even while it's fighting you (the threat colour used to win).

v1.3.2
- WoW Forever: raid markers show again. The game hides which marker a mob has from add-ons, so the plate now keeps
  Blizzard's own marker icon visible (the game draws it correctly) and moves it beside the name. /pn mark reports
  what it finds on your target's plate.

v1.3.1
- Quest icon: a grey ! on mobs that only someone else in your group still needs for a quest. Yellow still means you
  need it (WoW Forever full names are matched, and solo it's always yellow).
- WoW Forever: threat colours and the threat % show again when the game hides the threat numbers in a fight.
- WoW Forever: raid markers show on nameplates again.
- WoW Forever: mob levels are coloured by the Classic rules - grey only when a mob no longer gives experience
  (the modern game greyed out level 16 mobs at level 22).

v1.3
- Your debuffs in a row above each mob's name (WoW Forever: the game's own debuff row, kept up to date in combat).
- The mob's cast bar under its health bar: icon, spell, time left; grey when it can't be interrupted.
- Friendly plates (green): players and others on your side can get these plates too, with a green health bar.
- First names only: leaves off WoW Forever's second names.
- All four are switches in /pn.

v1.2.2
- WoW Forever: player nameplates show the full name (first and second).

v1.2.1
- A yellow ! beside the level on mobs you still need to kill or loot for a quest ("Quest icon on quest mobs" in /pn).
- Bigger plates by default: wider and taller bars, bigger name, health and level text (your own sizes are kept).
- The level number is sized to the bar.

v1.2
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Health bars and health text still show when the game hides the exact numbers.
- Tank colours work from your stance or form when buffs are hidden in combat.

v1.1
- First public release as Pint's Threat Plates.
- Threat colours for damage dealers and tanks (automatic in tank stances/forms), threat % and arrows,
  health text, level, raid markers, target edge, stacking and keep-on-screen, full colour options.
